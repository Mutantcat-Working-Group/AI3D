# Workflow references

The generation and review instructions were informed by the workflow concepts
in [blender-asset-workflow-skill](https://github.com/2409324124/blender-asset-workflow-skill),
reviewed at commit `8b9befa`: versioned baselines, saved-file verification,
numeric evidence limits, explicit reviewer independence and bounded repair.
The guidance in AI3D is written for its own generator and review service.

No scripts, scene files, example assets or showcase images from that repository
are bundled. Its repository-maintained content is GPL-3.0-only and its images
have a separate licensing scope; consult its LICENSING.md before reusing them.
AI3D retains its existing Apache-2.0 code license and third-party notices.

For a generated delivery, record any external models, textures and images with
their source, license, attribution and modifications. Keep those asset terms
separate from the application license. Import and metric checks are factual
evidence, not a certification of appearance or engine compatibility.
